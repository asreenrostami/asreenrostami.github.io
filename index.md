<p>
I'm a Senior Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) researcher at the
<a href="https://www.ri.se/en" target="_blank" rel="noopener">RISE Research Institutes of Sweden</a> Division of
Digital Systems, where I lead two strategic research areas: <em>XR Research &amp; Development</em> and
<em>Human-Centred Cybersecurity</em>. I hold a PhD in HCI from the Department of Computer and Systems
Sciences, Stockholm University (2020), where I remain affiliated as a lecturer. I'm also
<a href="https://www.digitalfutures.kth.se" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Digital Futures</a> faculty, and
affiliated with the <a href="https://stir.dsv.su.se" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Stockholm Technology &amp;
Interaction Research (STIR)</a> group.
</p>

<p>
My XR work looks at AI integration in immersive spaces, the role of AI agents in virtual environments, and XR
ethics. My cybersecurity work spans an ERCIM postdoctoral fellowship on &ldquo;humanising&rdquo; IoT security and
ongoing research on human-centred and gender-inclusive approaches to security &mdash; most recently a project on
how generative AI can be jailbroken and misused for social harm. You can read more about my
<a href="{{ '/research/' | relative_url }}">current and previous research</a>, browse my
<a href="{{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}">publications</a>, or see my
<a href="{{ '/awards/' | relative_url }}">grants and awards</a>.
</p>

<p>
I also serve on the <a href="https://imx.acm.org/sc/committee/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Steering Committee</a> of the ACM IMX conference (Vice President for Conferences), and on the ethical advisory board of an
EU-funded project. A continuously updated list of my projects and publications is maintained on my
<a href="https://www.ri.se/en/person/asreen-rostami" target="_blank" rel="noopener">RISE profile page</a>.
</p>
