<div class="resume-item">
  <h3>Jailbreaking AI</h3>
  <div class="subheading subheading-primary mb-1">Role: Principal Investigator</div>
  <div class="subheading subheading-secondary mb-3">Digital Futures (Seed Project) &middot; Partners: KTH, Stockholm University &middot; Aug 2026&ndash;Aug 2027</div>
  <details>
    <summary>Studies how generative AI systems can be exploited for socially harmful purposes &mdash; through jailbreaks,
  adversarial prompting, and other unsafe human-AI interaction &mdash; and aims to bridge technical jailbreak
  research with human-centred research on digital harm. <span class="read-more-label">Read more</span></summary>
    <div class="details-body">
      <p>The project treats this as a broader problem than cybersecurity alone, covering harms such as
      manipulation, coercive persuasion, misinformation, and intimate surveillance. Safety-aligned AI systems can
      still be manipulated into producing harmful or restricted outputs, and the bypass techniques that do this
      evolve and spread within online communities, often faster than detection efforts develop. Most AI safety
      work focuses on technical robustness and content filtering, while socially situated harms such as coercive
      control, deceptive influence, and AI-enabled interpersonal abuse receive comparatively little attention.</p>
      <p>The work combines expertise from human-centred cybersecurity, HCI, control systems security, and
      software supply chain security, running a shared program of exploratory research alongside four
      interdisciplinary workshops covering AI-enabled interpersonal harm, cybersecurity misuse, misinformation
      and manipulative communication, and ethical red-teaming methodologies. The aim is to map emerging misuse
      scenarios, identify research gaps across AI safety, HCI, and sociotechnical security, build
      interdisciplinary collaborations, explore ethical approaches to red-teaming, and produce an initial roadmap
      for future research.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>

<div class="resume-item">
  <h3>NextGenXR: Exploring AI Capabilities in Immersive Spaces</h3>
  <div class="subheading subheading-primary mb-1">Role: Principal Investigator</div>
  <div class="subheading subheading-secondary mb-3">Digital Futures &middot; Partners: KTH &middot; started Jan 2026, 2 years</div>
  <details>
    <summary>Builds on the earlier XR Horizons collaboration to study co-creation between users and AI, and interaction
  with AI agents in training and simulation, aiming to improve engagement, learning, and creativity. <span class="read-more-label">Read more</span></summary>
    <div class="details-body">
      <p>AI is now common in everyday tools, and XR hardware has become more affordable, but the intersection of
      AI and XR within human-computer interaction is still an emerging space, with open questions about
      interaction design, user experience, and the role of intelligent systems as collaborators rather than mere
      tools.</p>
      <p>The project studies how AI-driven agents embedded in immersive environments can guide, support, or
      adapt to users in real time, and examines the effects this has on engagement, learning, creativity, and
      decision-making. It develops and studies XR experiences that vary in visual fidelity, interaction
      modalities, and forms of AI embodiment, and runs user studies and group workshops on how people experience,
      interpret, and collaborate with AI agents &mdash; work intended to inform the design and evaluation of
      AI-enhanced XR systems for both individual and collaborative use.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>

<div class="resume-item">
  <h3>Virtual Training at Scale: XR, AI and the Future of Workforce</h3>
  <div class="subheading subheading-primary mb-1">Role: Coordinator, Project Manager &amp; Scientific Lead</div>
  <div class="subheading subheading-secondary mb-3">Vinnova (Advanced digitalisation) &middot; Partners: Volvo Trucks, SSAB, Luleå kommun &middot; started Sep 2025, 2 years</div>
  <details>
    <summary>Builds evidence-based frameworks for scaling XR industrial training sustainably across Swedish industry, with
  AI-powered virtual agents giving operators adaptive, real-time support on the job. <span class="read-more-label">Read more</span></summary>
    <div class="details-body">
      <p>Swedish industry must upskill new and existing employees as production systems and products change
      quickly, under pressure to deliver competence development on time while meeting high safety standards and
      productivity demands. Extended reality could make industrial learning faster, better, and more accessible,
      but adoption has been limited because there are few evidence-based frameworks for scaling XR and fitting it
      into existing organisational learning systems.</p>
      <p>The project builds knowledge on how Swedish industry can adopt XR sustainably and at scale, and develops
      frameworks for integrating XR into industrial training so it aligns with organisational goals, pedagogical
      principles, and technical capabilities. It also designs and evaluates AI-powered virtual agents that
      support operators with adaptive, real-time learning support in operational settings, grounded in the
      real-world industrial contexts of Volvo Trucks, SSAB, and Luleå kommun, with the broader aim of
      contributing to a more agile, inclusive, and future-ready Swedish workforce.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>

<div class="resume-item">
  <h3>AI-Enabled XR</h3>
  <div class="subheading subheading-primary mb-1">Role: Principal Investigator</div>
  <div class="subheading subheading-secondary mb-3">Vinnova (Innovative collaborations with the USA in XR, step 1) &middot; Oct 2024&ndash;Mar 2025</div>
  <details>
    <summary>Combines XR training simulators with large language models to move beyond basic text-based interaction
  toward a more dynamic, language-driven training environment. <span class="read-more-label">Read more</span></summary>
    <div class="details-body">
      <p>The project grew out of 2023 workshops and inquiries on XR and AI, which identified a need for a
      dynamic, LLM-enabled VR simulator that could support equitable education, help people overcome anxiety, and
      let them build skills through continuous practice. It draws on RISE's established EU and US networks as a
      basis for future cross-country collaboration.</p>
      <p>The goal is to build a working prototype that integrates XR and LLM technology &mdash; a basic XR
      training scenario featuring an LLM-based character for user interaction &mdash; form a consortium of
      collaborators to carry the work forward, and write a detailed report covering next development steps, key
      application areas, the challenges of language-specific training such as Swedish, and the ethical
      considerations involved. It is the first step in a two-step effort under Vinnova's Future Areas programme.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>

<div class="resume-item">
  <h3>Autonomous Last Mile</h3>
  <div class="subheading subheading-primary mb-1">Role: Coordinator</div>
  <div class="subheading subheading-secondary mb-3">Vinnova &middot; Partners: Stockholm University &middot; May&ndash;Dec 2024</div>
  <details>
    <summary>A speculative XR project imagining a future where autonomous vehicles have largely replaced human driving,
  used to provoke reflection on labour, safety, and the gig economy before that future arrives. <span class="read-more-label">Read more</span></summary>
    <div class="details-body">
      <p>Autonomous vehicles are widely seen as the future of transport, expected to be safer than human drivers
      because they don't get distracted or tired. In practice, deployed autonomous systems rarely take over
      human work entirely: they tend to automate the easiest tasks and leave people with the most complex,
      stressful, and time-critical ones, with remote teleoperation sometimes proposed as a fallback for
      situations that can't be automated. Set against a growing gig economy of precarious, often migrant,
      task-paid labour, the project builds an interactive vision of a future where these trends combine into an
      unequal, unsafe, and unwanted society.</p>
      <p>In the fictional scenario at its centre, a new operator is onboarded to teleoperate autonomous vehicles
      when a situation falls outside the system's abilities or legal limits, training on the same high-level XR
      headset used in live operations &mdash; and some training sessions are themselves live operations used for
      evaluating human-autonomy teaming. By letting people experience these pressured situations first-hand, the
      project aims to encourage designers, developers, and users to prevent this future rather than build toward
      it.</p>
      <p><a href="https://deltafleetai.github.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Project website: Delta Fleet AI &rarr;</a></p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>

<div class="resume-item">
  <h3>Multi-User, Multi-Sensory Interactions &amp; Ethics in Spatial Computing</h3>
  <div class="subheading subheading-primary mb-1">Role: Principal Investigator</div>
  <div class="subheading subheading-secondary mb-3">Digital Futures &middot; Partners: KTH (XR Horizons) &middot; started Jan 2024, 2.5 years</div>
  <details>
    <summary>Extends XR with multi-user environments, multisensory experiences including haptic feedback, and indoor and
  outdoor settings, with particular emphasis on ethics, security, trust, and inclusivity. <span class="read-more-label">Read more</span></summary>
    <div class="details-body">
      <p>XR can enable remote co-located experiences, realistic interaction, and support a climate-neutral
      society, but its effects on cultural and social behaviour, along with privacy and security concerns, still
      need attention. Current XR mostly supports a single user, especially when other people are physically
      present, and critics have noted that VR has largely been designed around a white, Western, male sensory
      body &mdash; leading to biased, non-inclusive design, harm to women, abusive behaviour, and a neglect of
      gender differences. XR also raises privacy concerns and questions about the unintentional participation of
      bystanders.</p>
      <p>The project creates diverse XR experiences and tests them with users in controlled experiments, varying
      social (single vs. multi-user), perceptual (no haptics vs. haptics), and environmental (indoor vs. outdoor)
      conditions, alongside group workshops reflecting on how multisensory experience and ethics should shape
      multi-user design. The work aims to examine how XR use relates to society's needs for ethics, security,
      trustworthiness, and inclusivity; to study users' multisensory experiences with haptic feedback both
      indoors and outdoors, as active and passive participants alike; and to derive ethical guidelines from the
      controlled evaluations and participants' own reflections.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>

<div class="resume-item">
  <h3>Understanding Real-World Cybersecurity Incident Responses in Sweden</h3>
  <div class="subheading subheading-primary mb-1">Role: Project Lead</div>
  <div class="subheading subheading-secondary mb-3">RISE Center for Cybersecurity &middot; started Feb 2023</div>
  <details>
    <summary>A pilot study of real-world cybersecurity incidents across Swedish sectors, examining how organisations
  respond during and after an incident, and how they reassess their security strategies afterward. <span class="read-more-label">Read more</span></summary>
    <div class="details-body">
      <p>The project tests the hypothesis that organisations which have suffered major incidents become better
      prepared for later ones, both through heightened awareness of vulnerabilities and through practices that
      help them keep operating during IT disruptions. It treats human, organisational, and technological factors
      as interconnected, drawing on behavioural science to study decision-making during incident response, in
      line with the team's wider human-centred cybersecurity work. The cases studied include a ransomware attack
      on a local municipality, high-profile attacks on multinational companies, and a medical records leak.</p>
      <p>The work is expected to yield methods and competences useful to industry and other stakeholders, and to
      support systematic learning from real incidents as a basis for further research with societal relevance.</p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>

<div class="resume-item">
  <h3>Plenary Multi-User Development Arena for Industrial Workspaces (PLENUM)</h3>
  <div class="subheading subheading-primary mb-1">Role: Senior Research Expert</div>
  <div class="subheading subheading-secondary mb-3">Vinnova &middot; Partners: Chalmers University (coordinator), AB Volvo, CEVT, Scania, University of Skövde, Fraunhofer-Chalmers Centre, Volvo Cars &middot; started Sep 2022, 36 months</div>
  <details>
    <summary>Builds a multi-user XR platform for collaborative 3D work on factory design, simulation, and up-/reskilling
  of operators, with KPIs for economic, environmental, and social sustainability. <span class="read-more-label">Read more</span></summary>
    <div class="details-body">
      <p>The platform lets stakeholders plan and assess factory layouts and collaboratively check whether a
      layout is feasible, and trains operators on future equipment, heavy and large-part installation, and
      interaction with collaborative and industrial robots. These activities run in a distributed online
      setting &mdash; a user's home or local site &mdash; with low entry costs and no need for travel or
      dedicated training facilities, drawing on research in XR, cognitive science, and computer-supported
      cooperative work.</p>
      <p>The broader aim is to help Swedish industry reskill and upskill its workforce at scale without being
      limited by existing factory space, and to produce technology and knowledge that can later be applied in
      other fields such as urban planning, public safety, healthcare, and occupational safety and health. The
      project supports UN Sustainable Development Goals 9 (Industry, innovation and infrastructure) and 12
      (Responsible consumption and production).</p>
    </div>
  </details>
</div>

  <h3>Gender-Inclusive Cybersecurity</h3>
  <div class="subheading subheading-primary mb-1">Role: Principal Investigator</div>
  <div class="subheading subheading-secondary mb-3">Digital Futures (Postdoc Fellowship)</div>
  <details>
    <summary>Brings marginalised perspectives into cybersecurity, starting with deviant use of smart home devices, to
  develop a human-centred approach to security built on diversity, autonomy, respect, and consent. <span class="read-more-label">Read more</span></summary>
    <div class="details-body">
      <p>This project is built around bringing marginalised perspectives into cybersecurity, starting with
      looking at deviant use of smart home devices. Its goal is to develop a &ldquo;gender-inclusive
      cybersecurity&rdquo;, a human-centred approach to security that encompasses feminist principles such as
      diversity, autonomy, respect, and consent, and applies them in a digital context.</p>
    </div>
  </details>

  <h3>Securing Things: Human-Centred Internet of Things Security</h3>
  <div class="subheading subheading-primary mb-3">Role: Researcher</div>
  <details>
    <summary>&ldquo;Humanising&rdquo; Internet of Things (IoT) security by understanding the technical, design, social and
  political issues that arise when considering IoT systems from a human-centred perspective. <span class="read-more-label">Read more</span></summary>
    <div class="details-body">
      <p>What does it mean to feel secure when using IoT systems? How do we design and facilitate secure IoT?
      This project's investigations took an approach stressing the relationality of IoT infrastructures, aiming
      to develop the cultural, political and empirical understandings needed to regulate, manage, and design for
      secure IoT.</p>
